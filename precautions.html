Stay Safe During Extreme Heat Conditions

Heatwave Alert Levels

Normal Conditions

Yellow

Be Aware

Orange

High Risk

Red

Extreme Heat

Do's

  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Wear light-colored cotton clothes.
  • Stay indoors during afternoon.
  • Use sunscreen and cap.
  • Eat fruits and vegetables.

Don'ts

  • Avoid direct sunlight.
  • Don't leave children in parked vehicles.
  • Avoid strenuous outdoor work.
  • Don't consume alcohol excessively.
  • Avoid dehydration.
