About Climate Intelligence

AI Powered Heatwave Monitoring, Prediction and Early Warning System

What is Climate Intelligence?

Climate Intelligence combines Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, weather forecasting, and historical climate data to detect heatwave risks before they occur.

The platform continuously analyzes temperature, humidity, wind speed, heat index, and UV radiation to generate early warning alerts.
